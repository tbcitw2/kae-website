About me

Hi, besties! I’m Kaelyn.

But you can call me Kae. I’m a small-town girl from Michigan who earned a business degree in South Carolina, graduated from Paul Mitchell, and then moved west to Idaho with the man of my dreams.

I specialize in seamless blondes, lived-in color and hand-tied extensions — luxury, low-maintenance hair that helps you feel confident every single day.

My promise: to be your biggest cheerleader, your therapist and your go-to hair girl.
