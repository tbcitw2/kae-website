Before printing

  • QR codes are placeholders. They point to a dummy address. Once the website is live, swap in the real booking link (or her Instagram) and test-scan every card.
  • Check the details with Kae: name as she wants it printed, whether to add a phone number, and the salon address (right now it says “Idaho · By appointment”, like the website).
  • Print files: for the print shop, export at 3.5 × 2 in with 0.125 in bleed and text kept 0.125 in inside the edge.
  • Mix & match: any front can pair with any back — e.g. 02 Midnight front with the 05 appointment back.
